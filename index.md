## About Me

I am a B.Tech + MS by Research student in Computer Science at **IIIT Hyderabad**.
My research lies at the intersection of **3D Computer Vision and Graphics**, with a
focus on **3D human motion**, **human-object and human-scene interaction**, and
**geometry-aware learning for AR/VR**.

I have published work at **BMVC (Workshop)** and **WACV**, with ongoing submissions
to **CVPR**. I am actively preparing for **PhD applications in 3D vision and embodied AI**.

### Research Interests
- 3D Human Motion Generation  
- Human-Object / Human-Scene Interaction  
- Geometry Processing & Mesh Simplification  
- Scene-aware and physically plausible motion
