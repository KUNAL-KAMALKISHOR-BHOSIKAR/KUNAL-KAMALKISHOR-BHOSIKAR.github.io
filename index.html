Kunal
Bhosikar

I am a Junior AI Image Processing Engineer on the Physical AI – Robotics team at Inner Sky Labs, Miko in Mumbai. I recently completed a dual degree at IIIT Hyderabad — a B.Tech (Honours) and an M.S. by Research in Computer Science and Engineering (specialization in Artificial Intelligence) — under the guidance of Dr. Charu Sharma in the Machine Learning Lab. My M.S. thesis is titled Towards Realistic Scene-Aware Human-Object Interaction in 3D World.

My research interests are physical AI, 3D digital humans and human–object interaction, sim-to-real robot manipulation, and robust and trustworthy 3D/4D perception and world models. At Miko, I work on sim-to-real transfer for robot manipulation and evaluate vision-language-action models. During my M.S., I worked on scene-aware human motion with grasping, co-speech gestures with object interaction, and single-image, language-driven human–scene interaction, as well as on the security of 3D/4D Gaussian splatting and JEPA world models.

I am actively looking for PhD positions in physical AI, robot manipulation, 3D digital humans, and 3D/4D perception.

Dense 3D Reconstruction via Stereo

Implemented Semi-Global Matching and PatchMatch for dense depth estimation from stereo image pairs. Evaluated on the KITTI Stereo dataset, analysing accuracy–efficiency trade-offs for large-baseline outdoor configurations.

Stereo Vision Depth Estimation KITTI

Imitation Learning for UAV Obstacle Avoidance

Implemented behaviour cloning and DAgger using expert velocity-obstacle-based policies for UAV navigation. Analysed generalisation and failure modes in cluttered indoor environments.

Imitation Learning Robotics DAgger

Dynamic Graph CL for Medical Report Generation

Developed a framework integrating dynamic knowledge graphs and contrastive learning for automated radiology report generation. Achieved state-of-the-art results on the IU-Xray dataset.
