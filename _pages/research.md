## Overview

My research focuses on modeling **physically plausible human behavior in 3D environments**.
I am interested in combining **learning-based methods** with **geometric constraints**
to enable realistic interaction between humans, objects, and scenes.

## Current Directions

- Scene-aware human motion generation with contact and grasping
- Human-object interaction with co-speech gestures
- Curvature- and appearance-preserving mesh simplification
- Geometry-aware learning for AR/VR and embodied AI
